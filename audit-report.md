# Audit Report – Botium Toys

## Objective
To evaluate the cybersecurity posture of Botium Toys and identify risks, vulnerabilities, and compliance gaps.

## Scope
The audit covered:
- IT systems
- Customer data
- Payment systems
- Internal network
- Physical security

## Key Assets
- Customer PII and payment data
- E-commerce systems
- Internal network
- Employee devices
- Warehouse inventory

## Risk Analysis
Risk Score: 8/10 (High)

### Major Risks
- No encryption for sensitive data
- No intrusion detection system
- No backups or disaster recovery
- Weak password policies
- Lack of access control

## Key Findings
- All employees have access to sensitive data
- No encryption for credit card data
- No IDS implemented
- No backup systems
- Strong physical security controls exist

## Conclusion
Botium Toys has significant gaps in cybersecurity controls, increasing risk exposure and potential compliance violations.
